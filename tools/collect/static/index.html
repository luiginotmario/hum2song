Whistle and sing a few songs

You'll hear the start of about 10 well-known songs. For each one, you'll either whistle the melody or sing it with any words you like, such as "la la la". Each recording is about 15–20 seconds. The whole study takes about 10 minutes.

Consent. Your recordings will be used to train and evaluate a song-matching research model, and may be published as part of a research dataset and paper. We store your Prolific ID only to process payment and do not collect any other personal information. Please don't say your name or other personal details in the recordings. You can stop at any time by closing this page.

Use a quiet room. Headphones help so the song preview isn't picked up by the mic.
